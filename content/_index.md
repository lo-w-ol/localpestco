## Termite services in Kingscliff

- **Termite inspections:** accessible timbers, subfloor areas, roof voids, moisture-prone areas, garden edges, fences, stored timber and visible termite workings are checked where access is safe and practical.
- **Treatment planning:** if active termites or conducive conditions are found, Shannon explains options such as direct treatment, baiting and monitoring, or treated-zone approaches where suitable.
- **Prevention advice:** you receive practical steps for reducing termite pressure, including moisture management, timber storage, access improvements and monitoring intervals.

## What your inspection includes

A termite inspection is not a guess from the driveway. It involves checking the building and surrounds for visible termite activity, termite damage, moisture sources, bridging points, timber-to-ground contact and conditions that can hide future activity. Where areas cannot be accessed safely, those limitations are explained instead of being ignored.

## Local risk factors considered

Kingscliff properties can include coastal homes, slab-on-ground construction, older timber elements, landscaped boundaries, damp subfloor pockets and holiday or rental properties that may sit unattended between visits. Recommendations depend on the actual construction, access, moisture and termite activity found at your property.

## What you receive

You receive plain-language findings, photos where useful, limitations noted clearly and a recommended next step. Local Pest Co does not promise a fixed termite treatment before inspection because the right approach depends on species activity, building construction, access and risk.
